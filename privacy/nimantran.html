Privacy Policy / गोपनीयता नीति

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · Contact: ervinfalabs@gmail.com

English

  • No account. Nimantran does not ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • Your photos and text stay on your phone. Cards are created on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. You choose where to share them (e.g. WhatsApp).
  • Content downloads. The app downloads new card designs from a public static website. This request contains no personal data.
  • Ads. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and usage data as described in Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Purchases. Payments are processed by Google Play. We never see your UPI, card or bank details.
  • Notifications. Festival reminders are scheduled on your phone; you can turn them off in Android settings.
  • Delete your data. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored by it on your phone.
  • This app is intended for users aged 18 and above.

  • आपकी फ़ोटो और टेक्स्ट आपके फ़ोन पर ही रहते हैं। कार्ड आपके फ़ोन पर बनते हैं और हमारे सर्वर पर कभी अपलोड नहीं होते।
